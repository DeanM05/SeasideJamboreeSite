Seaside Jamboree - Coming Soon

You've heard of a Game Jam... Get ready for a Jam Game!
Seaside Jamboree is a 2D side-scroller, cozy, idle faming game. Perfect for fans of Stardew Valley, Cookie Clicker and Kingdom Two Crowns; join our happy-go-lucky farmer on her quest to raise money for her dream wedding.
This relaxing, single player experience provides casual fun for gamers who are interested in the gratifying dopamine hit of watching that number go up. Whether it's easy afternoon fun, an escape for rainy days, or a time-killer during long queue times for competitive matches, Seaside Jamboree is a flexible experience for all types of gamers.
